Quick pro-tip

In Quiz 1 and in Lab 1, read the numbers before the words: an MTD (maximum tolerable downtime), an RTO (recovery time objective) and a WRT (work recovery time) must satisfy MTD = RTO + WRT, an address must belong to its subnet, and, when the ACL (access control list) holds Allow entries only, as in Kalmia's export, the effective access is the highest level granted by any entry that applies. Most wrong answers break one of these checks.
