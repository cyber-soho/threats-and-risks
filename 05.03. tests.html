Practice what Class 5 taught — before Quiz 2 and the midterm.

This hub holds the 20 flash-cards and the 24 practice questions of the four lessons of November 5, 2026: public information sources, CVE and MITRE ATT&CK, reading and rating a threat report, and building the threat catalogue. It is formative: nothing here counts toward your mark. Quiz 2 (Topics 1–4, Friday, November 6) and the midterm (Monday, November 9) are separate.

How to use it

  • Flash-cards: click a card (or press the space bar) to reveal the answer; arrows move to the next card; filter by lesson.
  • Quiz: one question at a time. Multiple-select questions say in round brackets how many answers are expected — select them, then press Check. Every option shows why it is right or wrong.
  • Use the Hint button before guessing: it names the passage of the lecture that answers the question.
  • The result page breaks your score down by lesson; start again from the flash-cards of the weakest lesson.

What the questions test

  • Lesson 1 — Public information sources (1/2) — cyberthreat newsletters and security advisories: 5 flash-cards · 4 multiple-choice + 2 multiple-select questions
  • Lesson 2 — Public information sources (2/2) — CVE and MITRE ATT&CK: 5 flash-cards · 4 multiple-choice + 2 multiple-select questions
  • Lesson 3 — Methodical analysis of public sources — how to read and rate a threat report: 5 flash-cards · 4 multiple-choice + 2 multiple-select questions
  • Lesson 4 — Building a threat catalogue for an organization: 5 flash-cards · 4 multiple-choice + 2 multiple-select questions
