One idea to carry into Quiz 2

A vulnerability is a property of a system; a risk factor is that vulnerability in its context. The same weakness is small on an isolated device and serious on a flat network, on the Internet, or on the roaster computer. Keep asking three things about every weakness: the evidence, the path, and the asset.
