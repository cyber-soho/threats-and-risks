Quick Pro-Tip

Before you classify a risk factor, ask one question: what must change to remove it? A physical item → material; a program, firmware or setting → software; a rule, document, role, contract or process → organizational; what people know, do or how many are available → human.

A risk factor is a fact with its evidence: asset — condition (source, location).
