How to use this hub

  • Start with the flash-cards: the definitions and the formulas (the “what”).
  • Then take the practice test: Kalmia situations that ask for judgment (the “why”).
  • A multiple-select question shows the number of right answers in round brackets, for example (3 answers).
  • At the end, the results show your score by lesson and every question to review with its explanation.

Quick tip

Before you choose a level, ask two questions: which evidence supports it (an incident, a report, a test, a catalogue entry), and which scale was approved before the assessment. A rating without evidence is an opinion; a rating on an undefined scale cannot be compared.
