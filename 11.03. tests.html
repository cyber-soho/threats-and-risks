How to use this hub

  • ✅ Flash-cards — the vocabulary and the rules: click a card (or press Space) to turn it; ← → to move.
  • ✅ Practice test — the reasoning: one-answer questions are checked as soon as you click; multiple-answer questions say how many answers to select, then you press Check my answer.
  • ✅ After each answer, every option shows why it is right or wrong. A hint is available before you answer.
  • ✅ The results page gives your score by lesson and the questions to review; you can retry only the missed ones.

Pro tip

Before any ranking question, write the keys in order: class → score → impact → criticality → threat evidence → readiness (LOW first) → identifier. Before any readiness question, add the eight scores and divide by 24. Before any impact question, compare the outage with the MTD (maximum tolerable downtime).
