Quick tip

Every preventive measure removes paths of attack, and every preventive measure is also a change that can break something. Choose the measures that reach the required level with independent layers, then assess and manage what each measure introduces. Most questions ask you to apply this to one of Kalmia's numbers: a deadline, a rate, a probability or a decision rule.
